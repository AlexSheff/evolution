The Evolution Chain13.8 Gya to now

Everything has a history.

A causal timeline from the Big Bang to post-biological intelligence. Each stage is a new way of storing, copying and processing information, built on top of the previous one. Every entry says what it made possible next.

Dates are approximate. Entries marked disputed are under active scientific debate. Part XII is projection, not history.

    Ages: Gya = billion years ago, Mya = million, kya = thousand. Source repository: github.com/AlexSheff/evolution. License: CC BY 4.0.
